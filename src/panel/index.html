    Settings

    A project is the set of images for one game. Link a folder from your computer, or a Notion page.

      Add a folder or an Obsidian vault

      Images stay where they are. Subfolders become tabs (Characters, Maps…), image names become captions. New files show up by themselves. An Obsidian vault is recognised automatically: images are grouped by the folders of the notes that embed them and named after those notes.

      Add a Notion campaign

      Every database on the page becomes a tab (one image per entry: its cover, or the first image on its page). Images placed on the page itself and its subpages go to "Pages".

      Notion token

      1. Open notion.so/profile/integrations, create an Internal integration with "Read content".
      2. Copy its secret (starts with ntn_) and paste it here.
      3. On the campaign page in Notion: ••• → Connections → add the integration.

      Other devices

      Only this computer can open the app now. Turn this on to show the players' screen on a TV or another laptop, or to run the master panel from a tablet. The master panel on other devices asks for this password.

      Devices on the same Wi-Fi can open:

        Stop the app

        Closes the app on this computer; the players' screen goes dark. Start it again from the app file.

        Master panel

        Enter the master password set on the computer running the app.

        The app is stopped

        You can close this tab. This page comes back by itself when the app starts again.

        Fog of war

        Players see every change as you paint. Hold Alt (Option) to paint with the other brush, X switches it, [ and ] change its size.
